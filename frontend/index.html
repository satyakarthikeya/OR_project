Operations Research decision support

Air cargo resource allocation and operational optimisation

Two optimisation models over one shared data layer, solved with PuLP/CBC and served by FastAPI. Load the dataset to begin; every later screen works from the dataset_id it returns.

Operations Research course project: FastAPI + PuLP/CBC + plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript. Dataset: Kaggle Air Cargo Resource Allocation Data (CC0), 5,000 synthetic records. Model formulations and stated assumptions are documented in SCOPE.md; every optimisation response carries the same caveat shown in the app.
